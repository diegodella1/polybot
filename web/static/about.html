About this experiment

An autonomous trading bot running 24/7 on a Raspberry Pi

Polybot is an experimental automated trading bot for Polymarket's BTC Up/Down binary markets (5-minute and 15-minute). It was built as a learning project to explore algorithmic trading, statistical modeling, and market microstructure in a live environment with real money.

How it works

Every 5 seconds, the bot runs through a full trading cycle:

1. DISCOVER
Finds the current 5m or 15m BTC market on Polymarket and subscribes to its orderbook
2. FAIR VALUE
Estimates P(up) from realized volatility and drift, finds edge vs market price after fees
3. RISK CHECK
Passes through 10 sequential risk gates including a kill switch and slippage monitoring
4. EXECUTE
Places an IOC order on-chain (Polygon), with fixed sizing ($1–$2 per trade)

At market expiration, winning positions automatically redeem $1.00 per share back to USDC.e, creating a self-funding loop. The bot learns from every trade through a k-NN pattern store and continuously calibrates its model using conformal prediction residuals.

Fair Value Model

Instead of predicting direction, the bot estimates the real probability of BTC going up using realized volatility, then buys only when the market underprices that probability (after the 10% fee).

COMPONENT FORMULA
σ (vol) std of 5-candle returns over ~1 hour, scaled by √(T/5) for longer markets
μ (drift) EMA of recent returns (α=0.1) + live 1-min momentum (15% blend)
P(up) Φ(μ / σ) — normal CDF, clamped to [5%, 75%]
Edge P(side) - market_price × 1.10 — only trade when edge is 6–20%
Calibration Conformal eq metric (p90 residuals) — triggers kill switch when model drifts

The model also applies temporal regime thresholds — requiring higher edge as markets near expiration — and filters out microspikes (sharp 1-min moves that distort signals).

Risk management

Every potential trade passes through 10 sequential risk gates. If any gate fails, the trade is blocked:

  • Bankroll floor — emergency stop below $2
  • Consecutive loss cooldown — pauses after losing streaks
  • Circuit breaker — shuts down if win rate drops too low
  • Max exposure — limits total open position value to $3.50
  • Position guard — only one position per market
  • Trade cooldown — minimum time between trades
  • Daily stop-loss — hard stop at -$25/day
  • Kill switch — multi-condition emergency halt (stale data, slippage, miscalibration, loss clusters)
  • Slippage tightening — raises edge requirement when fill quality degrades
  • Drawdown scaling — automatically shrinks bet sizes on losing days

Auto-tuning

Every ~12 minutes, an auto-tuning loop analyzes recent performance and adjusts parameters within safe guardrails:

  • Calibration tuning — tightens or relaxes edge requirements based on model accuracy
  • Regime edge tuning — adjusts per-timeframe thresholds targeting ~50% win rate
  • Active hours — excludes consistently unprofitable hours, rehabilitates recovering ones
  • Spread threshold — reduces max spread when wide-spread trades underperform

Decision algorithm

Every 5 seconds the bot runs this pipeline. Every gate is a hard filter — one failure skips the round:

1. Discover current 5m/15m BTC market on Polymarket
2. Estimate fair value: P(up) = Φ(μ / σ) from realized vol
3. Vol filter: skip if σ < 0.1% or σ > 0.4%
4. Edge = P(side) - market_price × 1.10
5. Edge band: only trade if edge ∈ [6%, 20%]
6. Temporal regime: require higher edge near expiration
7. Microspike filter: block trades against sharp 1-min moves
8. Conformal bound: conservative P(side) must exceed breakeven
9. Risk gates: bankroll, kill switch, loss streaks, cooldowns, exposure
10. Entry price must be in [0.35, 0.65] — near-50/50 markets
11. Spread check: reject if bid-ask spread > 5¢
12. Fixed sizing: $1–$2 per trade (adjusted for daily drawdown)
13. Walk-the-book: compute fill price from live orderbook depth
14. Execute IOC order on-chain (Polygon)
15. Monitor: stop-loss at -8% (walk-the-book exit) or hold to expiration
16. Winner redeems $1.00/share — no exit fee on redemption

Execution model

Orders are placed as IOC (Immediate-Or-Cancel) taker orders on the Polymarket CLOB. The bot uses a walk-the-book algorithm to simulate realistic fills by walking through orderbook levels:

  • Entry — walks the ask side level by level, computing a volume-weighted average fill price. If the book can't fill the full order, the trade is rejected (simulating IOC behavior)
  • Exit (stop-loss) — walks the bid side. If insufficient liquidity to exit, the position is held to resolution instead
  • No take-profit by selling — exits before resolution incur a second 10% taker fee, making early profit-taking almost always worse than holding to redemption

This means the primary winning strategy is: buy at a discount, hold to expiration, redeem at $1.00 per share (no exit fee). Stop-losses still make sense because recovering partial value (even with double fees) beats losing 100%.

Fee structure

SCENARIO ENTRY FEE EXIT FEE TOTAL
Win (hold to resolution) 10% 0% (redeem) 10%
Loss (hold to resolution) 10% — 10%
Stop-loss (sell early) 10% 10% 20%

The 10% taker fee is the dominant cost. At entry price $0.50, you need P(win) > 55% just to break even. This is why the model requires a positive edge after fees before entering any trade.

Directional asymmetry

Empirical observation showed that DOWN signals are noisier and less reliable than UP signals. To compensate, the bot applies a +3% edge penalty on DOWN trades — requiring stronger conviction before betting on a price decline. This reduces the frequency of low-quality DOWN entries that historically underperformed.

Paper trading mode

The bot supports a realistic paper trading mode (dry_run) that uses the same walk-the-book execution engine as live trading. Unlike typical simulators with fixed slippage:

  • Fill prices are computed from the real-time orderbook (volume-weighted average)
  • Orders are rejected if the book lacks sufficient liquidity
  • Slippage, fees, and risk tracking behave identically to live mode
  • Paper bankroll can be funded and reset independently from the admin panel

The goal is that paper trading results are predictive of live performance, so strategy changes can be validated before committing real capital.

The market

Polymarket's BTC binary markets are simple:

  • New markets open every 5 and 15 minutes: "Will BTC go Up or Down?"
  • Buy UP or DOWN tokens between 0¢ and 100¢
  • Winner pays $1.00/share, loser pays $0.00
  • 10% taker fee on entry — no fee on redemption
  • Settlement on Polygon network in USDC.e

Why it exists

This is not about making money. The bankroll is intentionally small and the primary goal is learning: how statistical models interact with live markets, how to build robust automated systems with adaptive risk management, and how to iterate on strategy through data.

The bot is constantly evolving — the auto-tuning loop adjusts parameters in real time, the conformal calibration tracks model accuracy, and every trade generates data that feeds back into the system. It's a live experiment in the truest sense.
