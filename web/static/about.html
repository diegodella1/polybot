About this experiment

An autonomous trading bot running 24/7 on a Raspberry Pi

Polybot is an experimental automated trading bot for Polymarket's BTC Up/Down 5-minute binary markets. It was built as a learning project to explore algorithmic trading, signal processing, and market microstructure in a live environment with real money.

How it works

Every 5 seconds, the bot runs through a full trading cycle:

1. DISCOVER
Finds the current 5-min BTC market on Polymarket and subscribes to its orderbook
2. SIGNAL
Computes a composite score from BTC momentum, orderbook skew, and RSI
3. RISK CHECK
Passes through 8 risk gates: loss streaks, exposure limits, circuit breakers
4. EXECUTE
Places a limit order on-chain (Polygon), sizes proportionally to signal strength

At market expiration, winning positions automatically redeem $1.00 per share back to USDC.e, creating a self-funding loop. The bot learns from every trade through a k-NN pattern store that feeds historical outcomes back into future decisions.

Signal components

SIGNAL WEIGHT SOURCE
Momentum 70% BTC price returns (1m + 5m) and EMA crossover from Binance
RSI 20% 14-period Relative Strength Index, overbought/oversold detection
Book Skew 10% Buy vs sell pressure imbalance on Polymarket's orderbook

Signals are combined into a composite score in [-1, +1]. Positive = UP, negative = DOWN. The bot only trades when the absolute value exceeds the configured threshold.

Risk management

Every potential trade passes through 8 sequential risk gates. If any gate fails, the trade is blocked:

  • Bankroll floor — emergency stop below minimum balance
  • Consecutive loss cooldown — pauses after losing streaks
  • Circuit breaker — shuts down if win rate drops too low
  • Max exposure — limits total open position value
  • Position guard — only one position at a time
  • Drawdown scaling — automatically shrinks bet sizes on losing days

The market

Polymarket's BTC 5-minute binary markets are simple:

  • A new market opens every 5 minutes: "Will BTC go Up or Down?"
  • Buy UP or DOWN tokens between 0¢ and 100¢
  • Winner pays $1.00/share, loser pays $0.00
  • 10% taker fee on entry — no fee on redemption
  • Settlement on Polygon network in USDC.e

Why it exists

This is not about making money. The bankroll is intentionally small and the primary goal is learning: how signals interact, how markets behave at the microstructure level, how to build robust automated systems, and how to iterate on strategy through data.

The bot is constantly evolving — signals get tuned, risk rules get adjusted, new components get added. Every trade generates data that feeds back into the system. It's a live experiment in the truest sense.
